Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Gigspree is a small, free app for musicians to keep their gigs, bandmates, money, guest lists and setlists in one place. This page explains what it stores, who can see it and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Your account: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo. With a passkey we keep only its public key. We never see or store a password.
  • What you enter: gigs and events, the people on them (names, and phone numbers or emails if you add them), clients and venues, payments, payouts and expenses, guest lists, lists, notes and songs.
  • To run the app: a sign-in cookie, a history of changes on each gig (who changed what and when), and, if you turn them on, the details your browser needs to send you notifications.

We don't use advertising, tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Who can see it

  • The people on a gig see that gig, as far as their role and the gig's settings allow (for example, managers choose whether players see the fee). People not on a gig can't see it at all.
  • If a manager shares a guest list link with a venue, the venue sees the gig's name, date and place and the guests' names. No money or phone numbers.
  • If you connect an AI assistant (such as Claude or ChatGPT) or a Siri Shortcut, it can read and change your gigs as you, until you disconnect it in Settings.

We never sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Google user data

Gigspree uses your Google account only to sign you in (name, email and photo). The app's owner can also connect their own Google Drive for nightly backups; this uses the drive.file permission, which only reaches files the app itself creates. Gigspree's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it's kept

Data is stored with Cloudflare, which encrypts it on disk, and always travels over HTTPS. Secrets such as connection keys are encrypted again by the app, and access tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. The app saves upcoming gigs on your device so it works offline; signing out removes them.

Keeping and deleting

Your data stays until you or a gig's manager delete it. Deleted items are hidden at once, but stay in the gig's records and history until we erase them on request. Nightly backups are kept for up to 60 days. To have your account and data deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, write to privacy@gigspree.in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will be announced in the app.
