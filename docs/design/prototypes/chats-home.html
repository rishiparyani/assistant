Gigspree, chats first

A clickable prototype of options 2, 6 and 7: chats are the home, answers in the chat work like small apps, and you can see the assistant at work. Made-up data; nothing is saved.

  1. Pull the list down for search. The two-dot button (top right) has colours and the Now / Polished switch.
  2. Tap "things need you" at the top and work through them.
  3. Open Building: its Chat tab lists its chats (Committee is private to 4 people); in Everyone, save Arjun's message as a complaint, open Visitors and add one.
  4. In Building › Flows, open Collect November dues.
  5. Open Assistant: tap the bars, change the split, try the suggestion chips or type "spent 450 on chai".
  6. Open Family and settle up.
  7. Tap your Y at the top of Chats: switch accounts, text size, language, and the first-run and invite screens.
