# Scorecard 2026-10-15: the setups models, side by side (first two of five)

The second round of the comparison (decision 2026-10-15 "The comparison's second round"): Haiku 5.5 answers at the everyday level and hands over; the tried model builds the setup. The seven level-2 cases (`suite=setups`), three runs each (21 answers a model), the same prompt, tools and scoring. Two of the five candidates so far; Gemini 3.1 Pro and 3.6 Flash wait on billing for the Google project, Kimi K2.6 on the Cloudflare `paid` gateway.

## The cases

| Case                   | What                                            | Sonnet 5.5 | GPT-5.4   |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------- | --------- |
| `setup-students`       | a new list with fields                          | 3/3        | 3/3       |
| `setup-card`           | a home card                                     | 3/3        | 3/3       |
| `setup-practice`       | a tracker with choices and a card               | 3/3        | 3/3       |
| `setup-field`          | a yes/no field on a list that exists            | 3/3        | 3/3       |
| `setup-field-hinglish` | Hinglish: a choice field with its choices       | 3/3        | 3/3       |
| `setup-view-filter`    | a home card with a filter                       | 3/3        | 3/3       |
| `setup-link`           | a new Venues list, and a link to it from Events | 3/3        | 1/3, 3/3² |

**`setup-link` was first scored wrong for both.** The check only read the first card, and both models put the link on a second card (`add_field` on Events) after the setup card for Venues. The check now reads all the setup cards (commit `49edf1a`), and the case was re-run three times on each model:

- **Sonnet:** on all six runs it made the Venues list and added the Venue link to Events, one answer, two cards.
- **GPT-5.4:** on two of the first three runs it made the Venues list and handed back without the link (a real miss: "link each of my Events to a venue" was dropped); on the other four it added the link. It also lists the collections first on every setup (one call more than Sonnet) and took 10 s a setup on this case.

## Reading it

- **Both build setups well.** Every field, choice, filter and card came out right on both; Hinglish was no problem for either. The one difference is that GPT-5.4 sometimes stops after the first part of a two-part request.
- **Sonnet is faster here** (5.5 s against 7.1 s median) despite thinking, because it makes fewer calls: it doesn't re-list the collections it was already given.
- **Cost is the same**, about ₹2.65 to ₹2.70 a setup. Setups are rare (a few a week for one person), so even a hundred a month is under ₹300 of the ₹2,000 cap.
- **Not yet:** Gemini 3.1 Pro, Gemini 3.6 Flash (Google billing first) and Kimi K2.6 (the `paid` gateway first). The table fills in as they run.

**For the owner to pick** once the other three are in. On these two: Sonnet 5.5, on right answers and on speed, at the same cost.
