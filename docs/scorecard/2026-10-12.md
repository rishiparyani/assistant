## What it says

- **Right often enough to be useful** for everyday requests (18/20), including money waiting for a tap, deletes waiting, totals, memory and handing setups on.
- **Slow:** 12 s in the middle, up to 31 s. Most of it is the model's own time on the free route, several calls a request.
- **Big prompts:** about 6,000 tokens per call (instructions + every tool), ~18,000 a request. Trimming the tools offered per request is the biggest lever for speed and cost.
- **Free allowance:** ~115 neurons a request, so the free 10,000 a day covers about 85 requests a day across everyone. Paid, that's about $0.0013 (₹0.11) a request.
- **Hinglish time words** ("sham" = evening) were missed once; worth a line in the instructions and more Hinglish cases.
