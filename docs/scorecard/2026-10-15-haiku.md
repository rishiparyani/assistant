# Scorecard 2026-10-15 (core and hard cases, Claude Haiku 5.5, three runs each)

The first of the small frontier models in the comparison (decision 2026-10-15 "Try the small frontier models"): `model=claude-haiku-5-5` on the Scorecard workflow, `repeat=3`, thinking on at the lowest effort, from the branch of PR #137 (runs 38736598222 core, 38736648353 hard). Level 2 not run (no AI credit yet). Next to the free model and Cerebras:

| Model                                     | Core      | Hard       | Hinglish   | Median | Slowest | Cost a message¹  |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------- | ---------- | ---------- | ------ | ------- | ---------------- |
| GLM 4.7 Flash (free, 2026-10-14, one run) | 18/20     | 12/34²     | 5/11²      | 2.9 s  | 21.9 s  | free allowance   |
| GPT-OSS 120B at Cerebras (one run each)   | 15/20     | 30/34      | 7/9 (hard) | 1.2 s  | 4.5 s   | ≈ ₹1.10 (trial)  |
| **Claude Haiku 5.5 (three runs each)**    | **57/60** | **98/102** | **33/33**  | 3.4 s  | 9.3 s   | ≈ ₹0.15 to ₹0.20 |

¹ A message is 2 to 3 model calls. Haiku's printed cost (₹5.38 a core run, ₹9.13 a hard run) priced every input token at the full rate; 88 to 90% of its input came from the prompt cache, which Anthropic bills at a tenth, so the true figures are about ₹3.5 and ₹6 a run (the pricing fix is in PR #137; the next run prints it right).
² GLM's hard run hit the free allowance halfway through; those are not the model's misses.

**Right every time:** 19 of 20 core cases and 32 of 34 hard cases, three runs each. Every Hinglish case, every date and time case ("parso subah", "aaj raat", "day after tomorrow, half past", "this Friday 4:30", "move to Sunday"), every money case, every "waits for a tap" case.

**What it got wrong:**

- `handover-card` (core, 0/3): made the spending-by-category query card itself, well, instead of handing over to setups. The same answer Cerebras gave; the case expects a handover. Worth asking whether the case is right: a card the everyday model can make on the spot is arguably the better answer.
- `open-reminders` (hard, 0/3): asked for the open reminders with `Done = false`, got nothing back, and said there were none. Cerebras did the same. Two models making the same call points at the tool, not the model: a reminder never ticked may have no Done value at all, so `= false` finds nothing. Check `find_records` on booleans before the next run.
- `tick-two` (hard, 2/3): once ticked all three instead of the two asked for.

**Speed and size:** median 3.3 to 3.4 s a case, nothing over 10 s (GLM: 22 s, and 65 s on 2026-10-13). Per request about 27,000 tokens in and 300 out, against 11,000 in at Cerebras for the same prompt: Anthropic's count of fresh + cached + stored tokens, to check against the console's own usage page before trusting it.

**Verdict for now:** the most right by a distance (98/102 on the hard cases, where GLM managed 12 and Cerebras 30), all Hinglish and date cases right, about three times slower than Cerebras and on a par with GLM, at roughly ₹0.15 to ₹0.20 a message on prepaid credit. The three misses are one judgement call, one likely tool bug, and one slip in three. GPT-5.4 nano/mini and Gemini Flash-Lite next, the same two suites × 3, when their keys are in; then the owner picks.
